01

NFC tag or Shortcuts automation not working

This is almost always one of three things: Bored Phone hasn't been opened once yet on the device (iOS needs that to index its actions for Shortcuts), the Toggle Bored Phone action wasn't added to the NFC automation, or the automation isn't set to Run Immediately. Include the exact step you're stuck on in your email and a screenshot if you have one.

02

Locked out and the in-app help didn't work

The locked screen has an "I lost my NFC tag" link that walks through recovery. If you've been through that and you're still stuck, email with your device model and iOS version and I'll help directly.

03

Bug reports

Include what you expected to happen, what happened instead, and your iOS version. Screen recordings help more than descriptions when the bug is visual.

04

Feature requests

Bored Phone is intentionally small. Not every idea will fit, but I read every email — send it over.
